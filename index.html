A Letter For My Baby Julie 💌

Every word here is honest, shy, thankful, and completely yours.

🔥
Forever & Always To: My Baby Julie ❤️
🪶

A Special Message Is Waiting For You

Click below to unfold my heart. I wrote every single word thinking of you.

My Baby Julie,

I just want to say thank you for today. Thank you for eating with me, for bringing me to the photobooth, and for giving me another beautiful memory that I will always treasure. Even the simplest moments become special when I spend them with you.

But, Baby, there is something I really want you to know. I notice everything you do for me. I notice how you treat me, how you buy things for me, how you always find ways to make me happy, and how generously you give without making me feel like I have to ask.

Honestly, I get so shy sometimes. You keep spending money on me and treating me almost every day, and sometimes I do not even know what to say. I feel so blessed, and I wonder how I got so lucky to have someone with such a beautiful heart. I hope you know that I never take any of it for granted.

Thank you for today's food, our photobooth pictures, your gifts, your generosity, and most importantly, your time. Thank you for choosing to spend your day with me. I know that money comes from your hard work, and that makes every single thing you do for me even more meaningful.

But, Baby, please remember this: you never have to spend money just to make me happy. Your presence already means so much to me. Eating together, laughing with you, holding your hand, talking about our day, and simply being beside you are things I will always treasure.

I do not just love what you do for me. I love YOU. I love your heart, your kindness, your sweetness, and the person you are. I want to be someone who makes you feel just as loved, appreciated, and cared for as you make me feel.

I pray that God blesses you for all the kindness you give. I pray that He protects your heart, gives you happiness and peace, and helps me become a better man who can love you the way you deserve.

Thank you for today, my Baby Julie. Thank you for making another ordinary day feel extraordinary.

I really, really love you. More than these words can explain, and more than my shy heart sometimes knows how to show.

Always your boy,

The one who is endlessly thankful for you. ❤️🔥
